What the player has equipped.

# `EQUIP_BOOST`

Booster v0.8.

Can boost upwards.

# `EQUIP_MAPSYSTEM`

Map System.

Can view map.

# `EQUIP_ARMSBARRIER`

Arms Barrier.

Weapon EXP loss is halved.

# `EQUIP_TURBOCHARGE`

Turbocharge.

Machine gun charges 2.5 times faster.
Spur charges 1.5 times faster.

# `EQUIP_AIR`

Air Tank.

Player is surrounded by a bubble underwater which prevents drowning.

# `EQUIP_BOOST2`

Booster v2.0.

Can boost in all directions.

# `EQUIP_MIMIGAH`

Mimiga Mask.

Triggers alternate credits illustrations.
Makes player use alternate sprites.

# `EQUIP_STAR`

Whimsical Star.

Spawns stars when the player's EXP meter is at maximum.
Also triggers the easter egg in the 'THANK YOU' credits NPC (ID 360).

# `EQUIP_COUNTER`

290 Counter.

Creates a timer in the corner of the screen.
